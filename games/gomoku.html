How to Play

  • You play Black, AI plays White. Click an intersection to place your stone
  • Get 5 stones in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win
  • Black goes first. The AI will try to block you and build its own lines
  • Click the board to place — works on desktop and mobile
