How to Play

  • Click Roll Dice to roll 5 dice
  • Click individual dice to hold them (locked dice won't reroll)
  • You get up to 3 rolls per turn
  • After rolling, click a score category to score your dice
  • Each category can only be used once per game
  • 13 rounds total - try to maximize your score!
