How to Play

  • You are the bottom row, AI is the top row
  • Click one of your pits to scoop all stones from it
  • Stones are distributed counterclockwise, one per pit
  • You skip the AI's store, AI skips yours
  • If the last stone lands in your store, you get another turn
  • If the last stone lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture it plus opposite pit stones
  • Game ends when one side is empty. Most stones wins!
