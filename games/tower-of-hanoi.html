How to Play

  • Click a peg to select the top disk, then click another peg to move it
  • Only one disk can be moved at a time
  • A larger disk can never be placed on a smaller disk
  • Move all disks from the left peg to the right peg to win
  • Try to solve in the minimum number of moves!
