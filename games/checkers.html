How to Play

  • You are Red, the AI is Black
  • Click your piece, then click a valid square to move
  • Pieces move diagonally forward on dark squares
  • Jump over enemy pieces to capture them (mandatory if available)
  • Reach the far row to become a King (can move backwards too)
  • Capture all enemy pieces to win!
