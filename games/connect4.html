How to Play

  • You are Red, the AI is Yellow
  • Hover a column to see where your disc will land, then click to drop it
  • Discs fall to the lowest empty slot in that column
  • Get four discs in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win
  • The AI uses a minimax algorithm with alpha-beta pruning for a tough challenge
