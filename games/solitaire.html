How to Play

  • Goal: Move all 52 cards to the 4 foundations (top right) by suit, Ace to King
  • Tableau: Build down in alternating colors (Red on Black, Black on Red)
  • Foundations: Build up by suit from Ace to King
  • Stock: Click the deck (top left) to draw a card to the waste pile
  • Click a card to select it, then click destination to move. Double-click to auto-send to foundation.
  • Kings can be placed on empty tableau columns
