How to Play

  • Goal: Move all 4 of your tokens from base to the center Home
  • You play Red, AI plays Yellow, Green, Blue
  • Roll a 6 to move a token out of base. Roll again on a 6
  • Move tokens along the path. Landing on opponent sends them back to base
  • Click a token after rolling to move it (when movable tokens glow)
  • Safe squares (star) protect tokens from capture
