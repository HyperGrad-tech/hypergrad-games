How to Play

  • Tap or click anywhere on the game area to switch your ball color
  • The ball cycles through 4 colors: Red, Yellow, Green, Blue
  • Pass through obstacles that match your ball's color
  • Hitting the wrong color or falling off = Game Over
  • The ball auto-moves up; obstacles scroll down
