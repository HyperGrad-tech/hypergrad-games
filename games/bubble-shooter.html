How to Play

  • Move your mouse to aim the shooter at the bottom
  • Click to shoot a bubble in that direction
  • Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them
  • Hover over the shooter to swap with the next bubble
  • Don't let bubbles touch the bottom line!
