How to Play

  • Use Arrow keys or WASD to hop the frog
  • On mobile, tap near the frog in the direction you want to hop
  • Cross the road - avoid cars and trucks
  • Cross the river - jump on logs and lily pads
  • Reach each of the 5 home slots to complete the level
  • Don't fall in the water and don't get hit!
