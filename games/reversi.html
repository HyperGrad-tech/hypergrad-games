How to Play

  • You are Black, the AI is White. Black moves first.
  • Click on a highlighted green dot to place your disc
  • You must place your disc so it flanks one or more opponent discs in a straight line
  • All flanked opponent discs flip to your color
  • If you have no valid moves, your turn is skipped
  • Game ends when neither player can move. Most discs wins!
