How to Play

  • Use Arrow keys or WASD: Left/Right to rotate, Up to thrust
  • Press Space to shoot asteroids
  • Large asteroids split into medium, then small when shot
  • Small asteroids give the most points
  • Don't crash into asteroids - you have 3 lives
  • Clear all asteroids to advance to the next wave
