How to Play

  • This is Misère Nim - the player who takes the LAST stick LOSES
  • On your turn, remove 1 or more sticks from a single pile
  • Click sticks to select them (all from the same pile)
  • Click "Remove Selected" to confirm your move
  • The AI plays optimally - can you find a winning strategy?
